## R00T96

The name R00T comes from *Person of Interest*, specifically Root's relationship with the Machine.

What fascinated me wasn't the Machine's intelligence or Root's ability to interact with it. It was their symbiosis. Through that relationship, both became something neither could have been alone.

96 has a different origin. It is my personal number, associated with safety and weight through numerology. I also think of it as staying below 100, leaving a margin rather than always pushing towards the limit.

Together, R00T96 represents a philosophy I carry into engineering:

**Human and machine intelligence can become more together, but capability must remain balanced by judgement.**
